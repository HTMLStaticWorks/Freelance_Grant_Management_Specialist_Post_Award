Mastering the Grant Lifecycle

From the moment of award notification to the final closeout report, I manage every step with precision.

1. Award Notification

Analysis of the Notice of Award (NoA), terms and conditions, and reporting requirements initialization.

2. System Setup

Configuration of financial systems, chart of accounts, and compliance tracking workflows.

3. Resource Allocation

Personnel effort tracking setup and sub-award management initiation.

4. Active Monitoring

Ongoing expense review, compliance checks, and budget burn-rate analysis.

5. Interim Reporting

Timely submission of quarterly/semi-annual financial and technical reports.

6. Final Closeout

End-of-project reconciliation, final financial report (FFR) submission, and document archiving.
