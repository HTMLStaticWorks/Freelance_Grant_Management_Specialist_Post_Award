Frequently Asked Questions

I specialize in federal grants (e.g., NIH, NSF, HRSA, USDA), state and local government contracts, and major private foundation awards.

I integrate directly with your internal finance and program teams via email, Slack, or secure document shares. Clients also gain access to my customized client dashboard to upload receipts, view deadline tracking, and access audit-ready folders.

I conduct comprehensive mock audits, trace sample transactions from general ledgers back to source documents, and identify any compliance gaps. I compile complete audit packages so your team is fully prepared for external single audits.

My expertise is strictly post-award compliance, budget reconciliation, and reporting. However, I often support clients by reviewing draft proposal budgets to ensure alignment with OMB Uniform Guidance and future reporting feasibility.

I establish a centralized compliance calendar tailored to your specific grant portfolio (such as NIH, NSF, or private foundations). I schedule monthly budget reconciliations and build alert triggers to compile and finalize reports well in advance of funder deadlines.
