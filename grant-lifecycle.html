The Post-Award Lifecycle

A structured approach to managing your grants from award to closeout.

1. Award Integration

Reviewing the Notice of Award (NoA), establishing chart of accounts, and setting up compliance trackers.

2. Budget Allocation

Distributing funds across program activities, personnel, and sub-recipients while adhering to donor caps.

3. Financial Monitoring

Monthly reconciliation of expenditures, ensuring allowability, allocability, and reasonableness.

4. Compliance Oversight

Ongoing risk assessment, policy enforcement, and sub-recipient monitoring site visits.

5. Reporting Cycle

Timely submission of financial (FFR) and programmatic (RPPR) reports to donors.

6. Closeout

Final financial reconciliation, equipment disposition, and long-term document archiving.
